According to the SSP-RCP scenarios, the future global LULC dataset from 2015 to 2100 at 5-year intervals are put in different folders.

"LULC Maps Color Legend.lyr", which can be used in ArcGIS, provides a color scheme recommended to the global LULC dataset containing 7 land types.

Land classification：
1	Water
2	Forest
3	Grassland
4	Barren
5	Cropland
6	Urban
7	Permanent snow and ice
